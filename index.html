Roadways are traced from the plan's double lines and drawn as 4.9 m × 3.7 m steel-arched gates following the Threequarter floor levels. Loader gates carry the conveyor (right-hand side facing outbye) and have an advanced heading ahead of the face; supply gates carry a rope-hauled rail track and no conveyor; which other roads were intakes or returns is not yet mapped. Rails and arches are illustrative, not surveyed. Worked-out panels (goaf) are not walkable.

Dated from the face-advance years written on the plans (516 readings). Ground not yet worked shows as solid coal; the latest three years are highlighted. All faces at Creswell were advancing: each panel starts beside the main roads and its gate roads are formed behind the face, so they lengthen with it. Main roads appear when the first workings they served began. Faces are placed square across each panel from its dated face lines: amber while working, grey once worked out. Loader gates run 30 m ahead of the face (advanced heading, Tee junction); supply gates finish flush with it.
